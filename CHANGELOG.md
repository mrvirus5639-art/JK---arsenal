# Changelog

## v0.0 — 2026-10-16
**First version: installed PC games on a shelf.**
- Finds Steam (all library folders, with Steam's own box art), Epic Games and GOG games, plus games in folders you add or single `.exe` files.
- Three display styles: spinning **Disc**, **Case** with the disc sliding out, and **Save** (floppy disk with a handwritten label).
- Covers looked up online for games without art; your own cover, rename and hide per game.
- Keyboard and controller (Xbox / PlayStation / Switch Pro) navigation.
