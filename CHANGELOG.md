# Changelog

## v0.1 — 2026-10-16
**Movies and Music, each on their own tab.**
- New Games / Movies / Music tabs (Ctrl + Tab, Alt + 1/2/3, or LT/RT on a controller). Each tab remembers its own style, sort and filter.
- **Movies:** scans your Videos folder plus any folders you add. Cleans up file names ("Interstellar.2014.1080p.BluRay" → *Interstellar (2014)*), groups `S01E02` episodes into TV shows with an episode list, uses `poster.jpg` / `folder.jpg` covers or finds one online, and opens files in your default video player.
- **Music:** each album folder becomes a CD in a square jewel case, with artist, album, year and track names from the tags and the cover from `cover.jpg` or the art embedded in the tracks. Built-in player with track list, shuffle, seek, volume and media keys; the disc spins while its album plays.
- Version number shown next to the logo.

## v0.0 — 2026-10-16
**First version: installed PC games on a shelf.**
- Finds Steam (all library folders, with Steam's own box art), Epic Games and GOG games, plus games in folders you add or single `.exe` files.
- Three display styles: spinning **Disc**, **Case** with the disc sliding out, and **Save** (floppy disk with a handwritten label).
- Covers looked up online for games without art; your own cover, rename and hide per game.
- Keyboard and controller (Xbox / PlayStation / Switch Pro) navigation.
